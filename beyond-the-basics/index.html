A.1 Which tool should I use?

Copilot gives you a few different ways to work with a document — and the right one depends on what you're trying to do. Before you start, ask: how am I going to use this file? Your answer points to the right tool. (The same thinking works for both Word .docx and Excel .xlsx.)
