Tips & Best Practices

Maximize Your Results

Write Clear Prompts

The quality of your prompts directly impacts the quality of Copilot's responses. Be specific, provide context, and include any relevant details about what you need.

Iterate and Refine

Don't settle for the first result. Use follow-up prompts to refine, expand, or modify Copilot's suggestions until they match your vision.

Review and Edit

Always review Copilot's output for accuracy, tone, and alignment with your requirements. Edit as needed to ensure quality before finalizing.

Explore Different Scenarios

Experiment with Copilot across different applications and use cases. Each app has unique capabilities that can enhance specific types of work.
