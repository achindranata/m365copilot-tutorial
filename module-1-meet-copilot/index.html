Meet Microsoft 365 Copilot

One minute on what Copilot actually is — then where to open it. A frontier AI model is only the engine; what makes Copilot Copilot is the grounding wrapped around that engine and the surfaces it shows up in.

SourceMicrosoft 365 Copilot overview — Microsoft LearnModule 1 · 1 of 6
What Copilot is — model, grounding & surfaces

Four building blocks sit behind every answer. Read them as one sentence: a frontier model reasons, Work IQ grounds it in your work, the in-app experience puts it where the artifact lives, and web grounding keeps it current.

The engine

A large language model

A frontier AI model does the reasoning, language understanding and writing — the part that actually produces the answer.

Your data

Work IQ

Microsoft's workplace-intelligence layer grounds answers in your emails, files, chats, meetings and people — always inside the permissions you already have.

Where it lives

In your everyday apps

Copilot shows up in a dedicated chat and inside the Office apps — working on the mail, document or meeting in front of you.

The world

Web grounding

Web search brings current, public facts that neither the model's training nor your own files contain.

Where to access it — three front doors

There are three ways to reach Copilot. Two are available to everyone with a Microsoft 365 sign-in; the third — Copilot working directly inside your documents — is unlocked by a Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium) license.

1 · Web Browser

Go to m365.cloud.microsoft/chat in any browser, sign in with your work account, and start chatting — nothing to install.

Copilot Chat (Basic) M365 Copilot (Premium)

2 · Copilot App

The dedicated Microsoft 365 Copilot app, pinned in Teams and available on Windows and mobile for Copilot on the go.

Copilot Chat (Basic) M365 Copilot (Premium)

3 · Inside Office Apps

Copilot appears directly in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams — working on the file in front of you.

M365 Copilot (Premium)

M365 Copilot (Premium)

With a Premium license, Copilot works inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook. Click the small Copilot button at the bottom-right of your document and the side pane opens — ready to draft, rewrite or analyze the file you have open.

Which one should I use? Start in the browser or the Copilot app for chatting, catching up and asking questions across your work — both are included with your sign-in. Use Copilot inside the Office apps when you want it to act on the document, spreadsheet, deck or email you're actively working on; this requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium) license.
