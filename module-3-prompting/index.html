Prompting Like a Pro

A prompt is simply how you ask Copilot to do something — create, summarize, edit, transform or catch you up. Talk to it like a capable assistant: plain, clear language, with enough context to do the job well.

3.2 From vague to valuable

The same task — preparing for an AI Engineer interview — gets stronger as you add ingredients.

More context → better answer
Goodthe task

"Help me prepare for an upcoming candidate interview for a new AI Engineer role in our IT department by creating a list of questions."

Better+ who you are+ why it matters

"I'm an HR Manager preparing for a candidate interview for our new AI Engineer role in IT. Please prepare a list of appropriate questions so I look prepared during the interview."

Best+ your sources+ the output you expect

"I'm an HR Manager preparing for a candidate interview for our new AI Engineer role in IT. Please review the candidate's resume here: /[resume] and the position detail here: /[position doc] and give me a comprehensive list of questions to assess the candidate's fit for the position."

Fun fact! — ask for the output format you want

Copilot doesn't only answer in chat — you can ask it to deliver the result as a ready-to-edit file in the format you need. Just name the format in your prompt and Copilot builds the document, deck, workbook or page for you.

Summarize this report as a Word document (.docx).
Turn these notes into a PowerPoint deck (.pptx).
Put these figures into an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx).
Give me this as a single self-contained HTML page (.html).

Name the format and the structure you want — headings, a summary table, a 10-slide deck — and Copilot generates the file ready for you to refine.

Power move! — let Copilot act in your other apps

Copilot, grounded in Work IQ (part of Microsoft IQ), doesn't only read across your Microsoft 365 — it can also act. Ask Copilot Chat to draft an email, set up a meeting or post to Teams and it does the work, asking for your approval before it sends, posts, changes your calendar or shares content. Multi-step actions in Chat are rolling out through the Frontier program for users with a Microsoft Copilot license.

Draft an email — "Send the summary above to a colleague" — Copilot drafts it and asks before sending.
Schedule a meeting — "Find 30 minutes with the project team next week and send the invite."
Reply in place — "Reply to the last email accepting the review and proposing Thursday."
Post to Teams — "Send these action items to our project channel."

Copilot drafts the email, meeting or post in the chat — you review it and approve before anything is sent, posted or scheduled. The action runs in Outlook, your calendar or Teams.
