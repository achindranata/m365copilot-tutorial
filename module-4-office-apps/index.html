Copilot in the Office Apps

In Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, Copilot works right where you do. The key idea to grasp first: Copilot can write straight into the file you have open — not just answer in a side pane.

Editing Mode — Copilot writes into your open object

Inside an Office app, Copilot can do two things: chat with you in the side pane, or switch to Editing Mode and put content directly into the document, email, spreadsheet or deck in front of you.

Ask in chat

Copilot answers in the side pane. You read the result and copy what you need into your file.

Editing Mode

Copilot writes the content straight into the open object — drafting a Word document, filling Excel cells, building PowerPoint slides or composing an Outlook email.

Microsoft 365 Copilot Notebooks

Microsoft 365 Copilot Notebooks

A workspace with a fence around it. You decide what goes in; Copilot answers from that and nothing else — so the same question gives you a project answer instead of a company-wide one.

Reaches for
Only what you put in it — files, pages, meeting notes, chats and links
Trades
Breadth for focus — it won’t wander into your wider OneDrive, mail or the web
Gives you back
Answers scoped to the project — plus audio overviews and a notebook you can share

A notebook is an AI-powered workspace scoped to one piece of work. You gather the content that matters — files, Copilot Pages, meeting notes, chats and links — into a single place, and Copilot answers only from the references you added. Nothing more, nothing less: a notebook doesn’t reach into your whole OneDrive, mailbox, Teams chats or the web unless you put those references in it. Add more as the project evolves and the answers update with it.

The scope is the feature. Ask this in chat and Copilot searches everywhere you have access to; ask it here and the answer comes from these twelve things — which is exactly what you want when a project has its own truth.

1 · Microsoft 365 Copilot app → Notebooks

Open the Copilot app at m365.cloud.microsoft and choose Notebooks in the left navigation. Don’t see it yet? Open the app launcher (the waffle at the top of the left pane) and select Notebooks — the entry point is still rolling out. This is the lightweight, chat-first experience.

Copilot Chat (Basic) M365 Copilot (Premium)

2 · OneNote → Copilot Notebooks

Open the same notebook in OneNote for the fuller, workspace-focused experience — sections, pages and free-form notes alongside your references.

Copilot Chat (Basic) M365 Copilot (Premium)

Two doors, one notebook. Notebooks stay in sync between the Copilot app and OneNote, so you can move between them without losing context.

Notebook or agent? A notebook is a scope; an agent is a role. A notebook is a container of references you curate for one project — Copilot answers only from what you put in it, and any instructions you set apply inside that notebook. An agent is a configured assistant: a name, a description, standing instructions, its own knowledge sources and capabilities, packaged so it behaves the same way on every task and shareable with colleagues. Reach for a notebook when the constant is the content; build an agent when the constant is the job. Notebooks → · Agents →

Example Use Case Prompts
Summarize every document in this notebook, grouped by theme, and highlight where the sources disagree.
Using only the references here, draft a one-page proposal with objectives, risks and recommended next steps.

Beyond Q&A: set custom instructions per notebook to fix tone and format, generate an audio overview to catch up on the material hands-free, and share the notebook so your team can co-create in it.

Availability & limits. Notebooks are available to Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Chat licensed users; the account also needs a SharePoint or OneDrive service plan. Supported references include .docx, .pptx, .xlsx, .pdf, .page, .loop, OneNote pages and notebooks, plus specific web URLs. Copilot grounds on up to 300 files per notebook — when you add a shared location such as a SharePoint site, it picks the 300 most relevant, so add a must-have file directly. Notebooks can’t generate images or charts inside the notebook, and deleting a notebook is currently permanent. Learn more →
